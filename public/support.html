Maintenance App Support

Support

The Maintenance App is a defect-management tool for approved Holcim UK Ltd employees and contractors, used to report and manage maintenance defects. It is distributed by invitation as an unlisted App Store app. The organisation responsible for the app and for personal information in it is Holcim UK Ltd.

Contact Support

Email: steve.pointon@holcim.co.uk

Getting an Account

Accounts are created by an administrator and you cannot sign up in the app. If you need access, ask your administrator or contact support. You will receive an invitation email with a link to set your password. Invitation links expire. If yours has expired, ask an administrator to send a new invitation.

Password Help

  • On the login screen, enter your email and choose Forgot password?
  • We email you a verification code. Enter it on the next screen and set a new password.
  • If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

What We Can Help With

  • login, invitation or password problems
  • changes to your role or the divisions you can access
  • problems reporting a defect or adding photos
  • unexpected app errors

Camera and Photos

If you cannot take or attach a photo, check that the Maintenance App is allowed to use the Camera and Photos in your device's Settings.

Information to Include in Your Request

  • your name and the email address you sign in with
  • a short description of the problem
  • screenshots, if available (please do not include your password)

Requesting Account Removal or Privacy Help

The app does not currently have an in-app option to delete your account. To request removal of your account, or to ask about your personal information, ask an administrator or email steve.pointon@holcim.co.uk from the address linked to your account, including your name and sign-in email. Requests are handled by an administrator. Removing an account removes your sign-in and access, but shared defect reports, photos and activity records may be kept and may still show your email address. See our Privacy Policy for details, your privacy rights and how to complain.
