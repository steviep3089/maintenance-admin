Maintenance App Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when you use the Maintenance App (the "app"). The app is a defect-management tool for approved Holcim UK Ltd employees and contractors, who report and manage maintenance defects. It is distributed by invitation as an unlisted App Store app.

The organisation responsible for your personal information (the "controller", referred to below as "we" or "us") is Holcim UK Ltd. You can contact us at steve.pointon@holcim.co.uk.

1. Accounts

The app is not open to the public and has no sign-up option. Accounts are created only by an administrator, who provides your email address, your role and the divisions you can access. You then receive an invitation email containing a link, which takes you to a screen where you set your own password. Invitation links expire, and an administrator can send a new one. You can also reset your password from the login screen using a verification code sent to your email address. You need an account to use the app, and we cannot give you access without your email address.

2. Information We Process

  • Account information: your email address, your role (user, manager or administrator), the divisions you are assigned to, and your invitation and sign-in status. Your password is held by our authentication provider, Supabase, and is not shown to administrators.
  • Defect reports: the details you enter, such as the asset, title, description, priority, category, division and status, together with the email address of the person who submitted the report.
  • Photos: images you take with the camera or choose from your photo library and attach to a defect or repair update.
  • Activity history: a log of changes made to a defect, including what was changed, when, and the email address of the person who made the change.
  • Technical information: authentication session data needed while you use the app.
  • "Keep me signed in" on your device: if you tick "Keep me signed in" on the login screen, the app keeps your sign-in session on your device so you do not have to log in each time you open the app, and remembers your email address to pre-fill the login form. The session is held in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain, or Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage) and is not included in device backups or transfers. The app does not store your password. If you do not tick the option, your session is held only while the app is running and you will need to log in again after it is closed. Signing out removes the saved session. If you share your device, do not tick this option and always sign out when you have finished.

We receive this information from you, from the administrator who creates your account, or from other users of the app (for example, administrators who update a defect you reported). The app does not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers.

3. Camera and Photo Library

The app asks for access to your camera so you can photograph maintenance defects, and to your photo library so you can attach existing photos to defect reports. Access is requested only when you choose to add a photo. We do not access your camera or photos in the background, and we only upload the photos you select. Photos are uploaded to Supabase storage and are shown in the app through web links, so anyone who has a link to a photo may be able to open it. You can withdraw permission at any time in your device settings, although you will then be unable to add photos.

Please avoid photographing people, or including personal information such as names or vehicle registrations, in defect photos where you can. Photos may also contain information stored in the image file, such as the date and time it was taken.

4. How and Why We Use Information

  • to authenticate users and control access to divisions and features
  • to record, review, assign and progress maintenance defects
  • to keep a history of work done and who did it
  • to send email: invitations and password-reset codes to you, and notifications about new defects to administrators of the relevant division
  • to maintain security, reliability and the service

Our lawful basis under UK data protection law is legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR): our interest in operating and managing workplace maintenance, defect and safety reporting, and in keeping a record of the work done and who did it. You may object to this use of your information as described in section 9.

5. Services and Sharing

Information may be shared with:

  • Supabase, which provides authentication, the database and file storage for the app
  • Email services (currently Gmail, and Resend for defect report PDFs emailed from the administrator portal) used to send invitation, password-reset and defect notification emails. Notification emails include defect details and the email address of the person who submitted the report.
  • Hosting providers (currently Vercel) used to run the administrator web portal
  • administrators, managers and other authorised users of the app who have access to the relevant division
  • authorities or professional advisers where required by law

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising or tracking. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics. The primary Supabase database for the app is hosted in the United Kingdom (London, AWS region eu-west-2). Our email providers (Gmail and Resend) and the hosting provider for the administrator web portal (Vercel) are global services and may process information outside the UK, and we have not verified where Supabase processes other parts of its service, such as file storage, authentication or backups. Where information is processed outside the UK, it is handled by these providers under their own terms and data protection arrangements.

This policy covers the Maintenance App only. Accounts you may have on other systems are separate and are not covered by it.

6. Security

Access to the app is restricted to invited users and information is transmitted to our providers over secure connections. We cannot guarantee that any method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please see section 2 about the "Keep me signed in" option.

7. Account Removal Requests

The app does not currently have an in-app option to delete your account. To ask for your account to be removed, or to ask about the personal information we hold, ask an administrator or email steve.pointon@holcim.co.uk from the email address linked to your account. Please include your name and the email address you sign in with. We may need to confirm your identity first. Requests are handled by an administrator and we will respond within the time limits that apply under data protection law.

Removing an account deletes your sign-in account and your role and division access. Administrator and manager accounts may need to be handed over to another administrator first. Removing an account does not by itself change or remove defect reports, photos or activity records, as explained below.

8. Records We May Keep

Defect reports, their photos and activity history are shared operational records used by your organisation. They may be kept after an account is removed, for example to show what defects were reported and what work was done. These records can contain the email address of the person who submitted or updated them, and removing an account does not automatically remove or anonymise that text. Whether an individual record can be corrected or removed will depend on the circumstances, including operational, safety, legal and audit needs. Administrative and security logs may also be kept where needed to protect the service. We have not set fixed retention periods for these records.

9. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law you may have the right to:

  • be informed about how your information is used, and ask for a copy of it
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • ask us to erase information, in some circumstances
  • ask us to restrict or object to certain uses of your information
  • ask for information you provided to be given to you in a portable format, where it applies

These rights are not absolute, and we may need to keep some records as described above. To use them, contact us using the details in section 12.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

11. Children and Changes

The app is intended for business use and is not directed to children. We may update this policy, and the date at the top shows when it was last changed.
